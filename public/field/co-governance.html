A commons, not a platform

pre-seed · provisional

A(DAI) is a commons, not a platform. We steward it; we do not own it.

Three things are fixed by design, and not ours to revoke:

  • The knowledge layer stays openly licensed — it cannot be enclosed.
  • The commons is forkable in full — every fork a legitimate centre.
  • No steward, including us, can privatise what is held in common.

Everything else is open, and shaped together: how the field is described, how contributions are weighted, how disputes are held, who joins, and how we are held to account.

Your work stays yours. The map is held in common.

We are inviting a founding cohort of artists, curators and institutions to shape this governance — not to approve it, but to write it. So this page is provisional. It will change as the commons learns to govern itself. That is the point.
