### Why the gaps

- **Collectives and platforms** (JODI, teamLab, Random International, 0xSalon, Hackatao, Feral File, Are.na, Art Blocks, Metalabel, etc.) are not `instance of: human (Q5)` in Wikidata, so the human-filtered SPARQL query skips them. A second pass with `wdt:P31 wd:Q16334295` (group of humans) or a broader filter would catch these.
- **No Wikidata entry yet**: many crypto-art and very young practitioners (Beeple, Snowfro, Matt DesLauriers, Matt Kane, Mitchell Chan, Mario Klingemann, Robert Hodgin, Robbie Barrat, Tyler Hobbs, XCOPY [has QID but no P18], Hackatao) either lack Wikidata pages or their entries don't yet have a P18 image.
- **Ambiguous labels** — "Kim Asendorf" returned no P31=Q5 match; the Wikidata entry likely has a different label. These need name-variant queries.

No duplicate titles detected across the 177-artwork preview vs. the 62 new artworks derived from new-entry `key_works`. If any surface later, the fuzzy-match layer in `build_seed.normalize_artworks` and `ingest_new_entry` will need tightening.

## Known limitations

1. **Auto-generated concepts are noisy.** Some concept names are paragraph fragments ("Institutional programme: commissioning"). A vocabulary-cleanup pass should merge similar concepts and prune fragments.
2. **`connections` fields in the 59 existing JSONs are free-text paragraphs, not lists.** The parser tries to extract clean name fragments but drops anything that looks like sentence material. This means some real connections are lost; conversely, some entries like "Trust Berlin" conflict with canonical "Trust (Berlin)" due to punctuation normalization.
3. **No institution nodes were created from exhibition references.** A follow-up pass reading `key_works[].description` for institution mentions would add EXHIBITED_AT edges.
4. **Lens drafts are not yet linked to any nodes via CLASSIFIED_BY.** The 5 `Lens_*` classification regimes sit in the graph but no edges connect them to content yet — that's an editorial pass for Iri.
